Where the panel gets its data

Coeno

Used for pages in Coeno mode, and for firmware updates and remote control in both modes.

Neptune Apex

Local login (the one for the Apex's own web page). The panel reads /rest/status on your network.

Hydros

Your Hydros app login. The panel signs in to the CoralVue cloud (the same way Coeno does) and streams live values. The password stays on the panel.

Pages come from Coeno

In Coeno mode the gauges and pages are picked in Coeno. Switch to direct mode on the Data source tab to pick them here.

Pages

Layout auto picks a grid from the number of gauges: 1 big, 2–3 side by side, 4 as 2×2, 5–6 as 3×2, more as a list.

Gauges

Readings appear here once the Apex/Hydros has been read. Normal range draws the dashed band and turns the tile to warning outside it; alarm limits turn it black, flash the screen and show the banner until you press the button.

Display

If the picture is faint, smeared or partial updates misbehave, try the other driver (restart needed). Check DIP switch 1 is on A.
